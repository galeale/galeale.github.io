Research in pictures

Four questions, one figure each.

ECHO CHAMBERS

Do social media lock us into echo chambers?

We compared how users cluster with like-minded peers on Facebook, Twitter, Reddit and Gab. On Facebook and Twitter, interactions were dominated by homophilic clusters.

The echo chamber effect on social media, PNAS 2021

MISINFORMATION

How did COVID-19 information spread across platforms?

We tracked COVID-19 conversations on five platforms, comparing reliable and questionable sources. Each platform responded to questionable content differently.

The COVID-19 social media infodemic, Scientific Reports 2020

CLIMATE DISCOURSE

Is the climate debate becoming more polarized?

Following the Twitter conversation around the UN climate summits from COP21 to COP26, ideological polarization grew markedly, alongside a growing climate-sceptic community.

Growing polarization around climate change on social media, Nature Climate Change 2022

ALGORITHMIC VISIBILITY

Can a platform quietly limit who sees your posts?

We introduced the p-score, a normalized visibility metric, to audit whether some content on Twitter/X reaches fewer people than its engagement would suggest.

Revealing the secret power, NDSS 2026
